# physics-project-

In our project, we plan to simulate a damped-driven pendulum, where both a time-varying external force and a drag force (dependent on velocity) act on the pendulum. 
This results in a second-order differential equation, which evaluated through two different method, the Euler-Crromer method (one we used for the drag force lab), 
and the Runge-Kutta 4 order method. Our project illustrates physical concepts such as air resistance, pendulum motion, and gravitational force.
